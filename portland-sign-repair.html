Commercial Sign Repair in Portland, OR

Dark letters, a flickering cabinet or a sign that won’t turn on? We troubleshoot illuminated commercial signs and help you decide whether to repair the existing lighting or convert it to LED.

Channel letters and wall signs

We trace outages in individual letters or whole signs, including issues with LED modules, drivers, wiring and controls.

Cabinet, monument and pylon signs

We check uneven illumination, intermittent outages and older lighting systems. Bucket-truck access is available for elevated signs where site conditions allow.

Repair or LED retrofit?

We check the condition of the sign and its lighting before recommending a repair or upgrade. Existing faces and housings may be reusable if they’re in good condition.

What to send us

Send the site address, a wide photo showing access, a closer photo of the sign, and a note about what’s out. A night photo is helpful for lighting problems. We serve Portland and nearby communities, including Gresham, Beaverton, Tigard and Vancouver.
